Unicode

As the Chairperson of the Unicode Emoji Subcommittee since 2020, I've had the incredible opportunity to nudge our global vocabulary forward, introducing little guys that seem to resonate, like Melting Face 🫠, Saluting Face 🫡, Face with Spiral Eyes 😵‍💫, and the complex feels of Smiling Face with Tear 🥲. What drives me is expanding our shared emotional palette online, giving us tools for the nuances – whether it's a flicker of confusion 🫤, a wave of tenderness 🥹, or the quiet intimacy of 🫂.

Advocating for symbols like Heart on Fire ❤️‍🔥, Lotus 🪷, or Face in Clouds 😶‍🌫️ isn't just about adding pictures; it's about ensuring emoji keep evolving as an authentic, inclusive, and deeply meaningful part of how we all communicate globally.

Emoji Kitchen

Emoji Kitchen is a feature developed by Google that allows users to combine existing emojis into unique, personalized stickers. Introduced in February 2020 as part of the Gboard virtual keyboard app for Android devices, Emoji Kitchen enhances digital communication by enabling creative expression through customized emoji combinations. The feature works by suggesting merged or elaborated versions of emojis based on user input. For instance, combining a smiley face with a cowboy hat emoji might produce a smiling cowboy emoji. These combinations are presented as stickers, which can be inserted into messages, adding a playful and personalized touch to conversations. As of July 2024, there are over 100,000 possible emoji combinations available in Emoji Kitchen, allowing for a vast range of expressions. However, it's noteworthy that flag emojis are not currently supported within this feature. Initially exclusive to Gboard on Android, Emoji Kitchen expanded its accessibility in September 2023 by integrating directly into Google Search across various devices. This broader availability allows users on different platforms to experience and enjoy the creative possibilities offered by Emoji Kitchen. Beyond Gboard and Google Search, several third-party applications have emerged, offering similar emoji-mixing functionalities. For example, the Emoji Kitchen app available on the App Store enables users to create personalized emoji combinations, boasting over 30,000 possible mash-ups.

Dynapuff

DynaPuff is a playful and blobby display variable font I designed with Toshi Omagari. based on the hand-drawn typographic stickers I made for Gboard. Its hand-drawn charm and bouncy baseline give it a fun, energetic feel, perfect for sticker art, children's books, and casual designs.

You may recognize it from TikTok, Capcut, and Instagram (until 2024). The font is a variable typeface with character-alternates that create a more natural, less robotic look for repeated letters so you can OOOoooOOo and aAAAAaaeeWWWee at it’s typographic tone of voice.

Animated Emoji

Google's Noto Emoji project has introduced a collection of animated emojis, enhancing digital communication with dynamic expressions. This animated subset includes 180 different designs, covering categories such as smileys, people, animals, and objects. These animations are integrated into platforms like Google Messages, where sending a single emoji can trigger expressive animations, adding a playful element to conversations. Users have noted that the feature is accessible when using Google Messages and may require specific settings adjustments, such as ensuring that the "Remove Animations" option is toggled off in accessibility settings.
